Getting there

Recommended

By car

Bowral is roughly 1.5 hours from Sydney CBD via the Hume Motorway (M31). Free on-site parking is available at Milton Park.

By train

The Southern Highlands line runs to Bowral station. It's a short taxi or rideshare from there to Milton Park. Rideshare in the Highlands can be thin, so book a local taxi in advance.

Getting home

The celebration wraps up at 11:00 pm. Please organise your ride home before the day (local taxis book out, so reserve early), or stay the night on site.

Please don't drink and drive.

Formal

Gentlemen

Ladies

Floor-length or elegant midi dresses, or a smart formal jumpsuit.

Please avoid

FAQs

Can I bring a plus-one?

We're keeping our celebration intimate, so we're unable to accommodate plus-ones. Only the guests named on your invitation are invited.

Are kids invited?

We love your little ones, but our wedding is an adults-only celebration. We hope this gives you a night off to relax and celebrate with us!

Will the ceremony be unplugged?

Yes. We have a photographer and videographer capturing everything, so we'd love you to put your phones away during the ceremony and just be present with us. Snap away afterwards!

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking is available on site at Milton Park.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 2:30 pm so we can begin the ceremony right at 3:00 pm.

Can I stay at the venue?

Yes! Book with promo code WED01NOV27 using the link in Where to Stay.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves indoors to the Elms room. Everything else stays the same.

Gifts?

Your presence is truly the greatest gift. If you would like to give something, we'll have a wishing well at the reception.
